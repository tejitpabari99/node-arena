## Tower War - visual breakdown
- Camera: fixed high-angle perspective (~60-70deg pitch), portrait, slight perspective; whole level fits one screen. Tilted top-down, towers seen from front-above.
- Ground: flat themed tile-less diorama (grass #7CC52E w/ darker green shadows; sand #F3C26B desert; icy blue #BFE6F2 snow; purple-dark cave #3A2A4A; lava). Themed border props outside play area (rocks, crates, tyres, rails, snowmen, crystals). Soft baked blobs shadows. Low-poly toy look, no outlines.
- Palette: blue #2F7BE0 (player), red #E53935, green #3DBE3D, yellow/orange #F5A623, neutral gray #8E8E93. Towers: coloured top cap/roof + white/grey stone body.
- Towers: stacked octagonal/boxy tiers; level shown by HEIGHT: more stacked tiers (1 tier ~lvl<10, 2 ~10-19, 3-4 higher); small tier with arched door at base. Types: barracks (stacked tower), factory (low sheds w/ smokestack, "up-arrow N" label = spawn count), fort (4x size), neutral lookout hut (stilts + pyramid roof = gun tower, gray), sniper, rocket launcher. Gold bar piles = pickups with number.
- Count label: white bold number w/ dark outline floating directly ABOVE tower top, ~35-45% of tower width tall; "Max" text at cap (63). Under/above number: 1-3 dots (route slots: filled white = free, translucent = in use). Lvl 0 shows "0".
- Lines: flat translucent ribbon laid on ground, straight, width ~ 1/4-1/3 of tower width, team colour at ~50% opacity (blue #6FB3E8-ish, red #FF8A80-ish), with tiny dotted/dashed centre pattern and soft edges; no bridge unless gap (bridge built by troops, slows). Line ends at tower base. Direction not shown with arrows (just troop movement); small up-arrow icons by tower show upgrades available.
- Troops: tiny chibi soldiers (1 unit = 1 soldier), marching in single file/loose stream on ribbon; tanks (factory) count as 2. Team-coloured helmets. Stream, not one blob; count not shown per line (just individual units).
- Clash: opposing lines crossing/overlapping shown simply as two translucent ribbons overlapping (see 06, yt). Units meeting fight at the point they collide on the lane, small coloured particle splats (green/red confetti blobs) [unverified: exact 1v1 annihilation rule].
- HUD: top-left undo/restart icon, top-center level bar (progress pill split by team colours + level number), top-right coins, bottom-left X3 BOOST (ad), bottom tutorial hints. White translucent circle on ground = gun range.

## City Takeover - visual breakdown
- Camera: near top-down tilted (~70-75deg), portrait, minimal perspective; level centred on pale ground.
- Ground: pale yellow-cream #F2F2B8 sand w/ faint checker/diamond tint (menus) or snow #DCEEF5 (lvl10); river curve cyan #17D0F0 at bottom, round low-poly trees (#5BAA3A / #3F8E2F) forming border, blob shadows. Very flat colors, no outlines, low-poly.
- Palette: blue #1E9BFF, red #F5213A, green #2FB84A, yellow #F7D227, neutral gray #A8ABB0, walls dark charcoal #3A3A3F.
- Buildings: 3 shapes = box skyscraper (slim, stripes = floors; "normal"), cylinder disk stack (circle = heal/defend, shield icons), triangle/pyramid stack (attack, sword icons). Level shown by number of stacked slabs (taller stack = more pop); facade lines (window slits). Gray neutral variants share shapes. Box ~2x1.5 units, roughly height 2-3x width for big ones.
- Count label: huge white bold number w/ black outline sitting ON TOP, width ~80-90% of building width; below it small row of dots/icons (1-3 white circles = lane slots; shield/sword icons = cylinder/triangle type).
- Lanes (roads): straight flat ribbon, width ~25-30% of building width, light team tint (#9FD0F5 blue, #F5A5AC red, yellow #F7E27A), with white triangular chevron arrows repeating along lane showing direction; ends hidden under building. Opposing teams' lanes between same pair drawn as two lanes side by side/overlapping; lanes cross over each other freely (no junction).
- Troops: tiny chibi characters (blue: orange-haired w/ blue outfits; red: chicken-like/white w/ red hearts) walking single file; as they meet, small melee cluster with sword/heart icons; fights occur at meeting point on lane [partly verified: seen in screenshot 01].
- Clash: crossing lanes, troops meet and trade in place; no tug-of-war bar visible. [unverified rules]
- HUD: top-left undo arrow, top-center banner "LEVEL N" split into team colour segments (blue|red|green|yellow proportional?), bolted corners; menu: ticket/gem/coin pills top.
- Walls: black bars rect blocking lane drawing; river as decor.

## Mechanics (sourced)
Tower War (https://www.levelwinner.com/tower-war-tactical-conquest-beginners-guide-tips-tricks-strategies/ ; https://writerparty.com/party/tower-war-tactical-conquest-walkthrough-tips-cheats-and-strategy-guide/ ; https://apps.apple.com/app/id1579356887):
- Swipe from own tower to target = straight line, troops flow continuously. Swipe across an existing line to CUT it (frees slot).
- Number on tower = level/troop count; lower enemy to 0 -> captured. Arriving friendly troops raise level. Max 63 (fort higher); at max, troops pass through. Towers with no outgoing path accumulate own generated troops.
- Route slots: 1 default, 2nd at level 10, 3rd at 20 (dots on tower). Barracks: infantry (1), Factory: tanks (=2 soldiers), Fort: parachutes constant, 4x size. Sniper/rocket/gun towers; neutral gray towers attack all colours.
- Obstacles: wooden crates must be destroyed; gaps -> troops build bridge (slow); mines kill units.
- Boost (ad) = 3x generation. Gold bars speed growth. Air support sets random enemy tower to 0.
- AI: campaign bots attack each other as well as player; PvP "opponents" are bots w/ visible stats.
- Head-on clash rules: NOT documented in sources [unverified]; visually, troops from opposing lines that overlap fight on the lane.
City Takeover (https://apps.apple.com/app/id1538467877 ; https://www.crazygames.com/game/city-takeover [via fetch summary] ; https://www.gamezebo.com/walkthroughs/city-takeover-strategy-guide-the-best-hints-tips-and-cheats/ [403, search snippet only]):
- Drag finger from your building to another to connect; one building may have several paths; dragging to a connected building / cutting stops the flow. "Connect buildings to grow them taller."
- Population grows over time per building, flows along paths; outnumber defenders to capture and gain population.
- Triangles = attack (damage enemy buildings faster); circles/cylinders = reinforce/heal allies, build own faster; boxes = standard.
- Neutral gray buildings capture early; walls appear later; freeze-time via ad. Largest tower draws opponent defence.
- Teams: blue(you), red, yellow, green.
- Clash rule: undocumented [unverified].

## Recommendations (inferred)
- Use tilted-top-down camera, flat pastel diorama, blob shadows, chunky low-poly.
- Level = stack height + big outlined white number above; slot dots below.
- Lines = translucent team-tinted ribbons w/ chevrons (CT) or dotted (TW); opposing lines overlap as separate ribbons.
- Troops = tiny chibi figures in single file.
