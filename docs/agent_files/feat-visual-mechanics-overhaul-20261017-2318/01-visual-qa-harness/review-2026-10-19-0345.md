## Must-fix
- [Bug Hunter, Architect] apps/web/visual/lib/pixel-metrics.mjs:22 `REF_TOWER_WIDTH = 67`; thresholds.json M16 (11 / 9 px @768); PRD.md M16 row (:98) and step 4a (:109); CHECKLIST.md M16 row and Appendix B; iteration-log row 21. The M16 floor is derived at the M01 band max instead of at the refs' own viewport-height share.
  - **The window.** At the refs' digit ratio (min 0.164, which is also the V07 "= yt1 38" anchor), a tower needs >= 67.07 px to reach 11 px. M01 allows at most 67.2 px. At 1080 the same holds: 15.47 / 0.164 = 94.3 px against a 94.5 px max. The joint window for a ref-like tower is a 0.2% knife edge at the band ceiling.
  - **Worse in practice.** M16 is a min over every tower, so far or perspective-shrunk towers would all have to sit at or above the band max. That pushes the median past the M01 max.
  - **Refs at their own size.** tw01 ~5.8-6.25% vh and yt1 ~7% vh give 45-54 px at 768, and glyphs of about 7.3-9.2 px, which fails 11. The refs pass calibrate only because their crops are scaled up to the band max.
  - **The claim.** The commit message, PRD and iteration log say M01/M16/V07 "stay jointly satisfiable". That holds only for digit ratios well above the refs' own. For example, ratio 0.22 needs >= 50 px (6.5% vh).
  - **Why it matters.** SP03 will fail M16 on a ref-faithful camera, for reasons the docs claim do not exist.
  - **Fix option A.** Rescale each ref crop to its own M01 share at 768 (tw01 ~45-48 px, yt1 ~54 px), or to the band floor. Set the M16 floor to the min glyph at that size (expect ~7-8 px @768). Re-derive L18 the same way, then re-run `visual:calibrate`.
  - **Fix option B.** Keep 67 px, record the near-point joint window as a PRD Open Question for a user decision, and remove the "jointly satisfiable" claim.
  - Either way, update the REF_TOWER_WIDTH unit test and doc comment together.
- [Architect; evidence also from Bug Hunter] iteration-log.md row 21 resample sweep; PRD.md step 4a. The 11 px floor sits inside the measurement noise.
  - **Evidence.** The refs pass at 66/67 px and fail at 64/68/70 px. yt1 "44" drops to 9 px and M02 to 0.132, because the yt1 video-soft digit jitters by +-2 px with crop size.
  - **Why it matters.** A floor that both refs pass at only one chosen crop size is fitted to that size, not calibrated. Step 4a's rule ("any ref failure means the metric is wrong") is met only by picking the passing resample size. SP03 captures near real tower sizes will get false failures.
  - **Fix.** Set the M16 floor below the ref minimum across a size sweep around the chosen base, for example +-2-3 px. Document that noise margin in the M16 Calibration column.
