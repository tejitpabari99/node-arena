## Closest matches
1. Tower Battle: Connect Towers - only one with good visuals + levels + browser + (claimed) PvP/friends. Misses: ads, friends private mode unproven, may be random PvP.
2. Tentacle Wars (iOS) - true persistent links, 80 levels, friend invite. Misses: 1v1 only, iOS-only MP and reportedly buggy, ads/IAP, no browser, not tower/marching-unit visuals.
3. BitPlanets - persistent streams, browser, multiplayer. Misses: circle-style visuals, no levels, ads (changelog), private rooms unconfirmed.
Honorable: Mushroom Wars 2 / Auralux meet 3,4,5 and (MW2) 2, but not persistent routes.

## Gaps
No product found with all of: persistent routes + tower/unit visuals + private 2-4 friends lobby + level picker + zero ads + browser. Persistent-route games skew single-player mobile with ads; friend-lobby games (Mushroom Wars 2, Auralux) use manual sends.

## Verdict
Nothing verified meets all must-haves. Only worthwhile pre-build check: hands-on test Tower Battle (browser) for private friend rooms; even if present, it has ads. Recommendation: build your own (persistent-route + level picker + room-code friends mode in browser is an open niche). Cheap validation: play Tower Battle and BitPlanets ~15 min each first.
